“If you are not going to tell the world who you are, the world is not going to tell you, how good you are.”

These days I work at the frontier of AI engineering — building agents, automation and AI-powered workflows across engineering repos and teams, with a focus on context and harness engineering and hands-on R&D to make LLMs reliable in real codebases.
